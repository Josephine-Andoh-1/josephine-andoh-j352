Candidates for California gubernatorial race undecided, poll finds

By Josephine Andoh

April 20, 2026

A plurality of voters are unsure of their preferred candidate for the June 2 primaries for California's gubernatorial election, according to a survey conducted from April 14 to 15 by Emerson College Polling and Inside California Politics. The poll, the first to be conducted since former Rep. Eric Swalwell (D-CA) dropped out of the race on April 12 due to sexual assault allegations, found that 23% of likely voters are undecided. The candidate with the highest share of the vote is Republican Steve Hilton with 16.6%.
